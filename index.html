About the group

Learning together by building something real.

UAE Tech Skills & Projects is a voluntary collaborative learning and portfolio-building group. The aim is to bridge career and experience gaps by working like a real cross-functional technology team, not by studying in isolation.

01 · REAL BUSINESS CONTEXT

Problem before technology

We first identify a genuine UAE business problem, target users and measurable pain points before deciding what to build.

02 · CROSS-FUNCTIONAL DELIVERY

One project, many disciplines

Business analysis, product, software, data, Power BI, AI/ML, cloud, QA and project management work together in one delivery cycle.

03 · CAREER EVIDENCE

Build proof, not only certificates

The finished work should become practical evidence for GitHub, LinkedIn, interviews, case studies and future UAE opportunities.

The team is currently mapping skills, preferred domains and access to real subject-matter experts. The next decision is to select a UAE-relevant domain and validate one concrete business problem before defining the final product or service.

Current decision criteria

  • Is the problem relevant to the UAE market?
  • Can we speak to a business owner, professional or subject-matter expert?
  • Does it cover important skill gaps across the team?
  • Can we deliver a credible MVP in a manageable timeframe?
  • Will it create strong portfolio and interview evidence?
  • Could it later become a useful product or implementation service?

How we will work

Our project strategy

The team will move from business discovery to a validated MVP, then turn the result into a reusable portfolio and career asset.

Map the team

Confirm the core team, availability, preferred roles, backup roles and learning goals.

Select a domain

Compare domains against UAE opportunity, team fit, access to domain knowledge and MVP feasibility.

Validate a problem

Interview a business owner or subject-matter expert and document who has the problem, how it is handled today and why it is inefficient.

Define the MVP

Create a problem statement, personas, requirements, user stories, storyboard, data needs, architecture and success measures.

Build in sprints

Use GitHub, weekly meetings, task ownership, code reviews, testing and demo checkpoints to work like a real delivery team.

Showcase the outcome

Deploy the product, publish documentation, dashboards and demo material, and create individual portfolio evidence for each contributor.
